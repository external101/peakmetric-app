Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Peakmetric.

How does Peakmetric connect to my TikTok account?
Peakmetric uses TikTok's official Login Kit to securely connect to your account. You authorize access through TikTok's own sign-in page, and we never see or store your password. We access your profile information, follower statistics, and video data through authorized API scopes.
What data does Peakmetric access?
We access your basic profile information, follower and engagement statistics, and your public video list. This powers our engagement scoring, growth tracking, and video analytics features. We do not post content or make changes to your account.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your data with third parties. You can revoke access and delete your data at any time from your account settings. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.
Can I use Peakmetric for free?
Yes. Our Free plan gives you access to one TikTok account with an engagement score, 7 days of performance history, and basic video analytics. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited accounts, full history, and exportable reports.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your account settings. Your account reverts to the Free plan at the end of the billing cycle. There are no cancellation fees or penalties.
