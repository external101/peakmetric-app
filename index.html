Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Peakmetric.

How does Peakmetric connect to my TikTok?
We use TikTok's official Login Kit for secure OAuth authentication. You sign in with TikTok, and we receive read-only access to your profile, stats, and video list. We never see your password.
What is the engagement score?
The engagement score is a composite metric that considers your average likes, comments, shares, and views relative to your follower count. It gives you one number that summarizes how well your content performs.
Can brands see my Peakmetric profile?
Only if you share it. You can export a PDF or CSV report and send it to brands directly. Your data is private by default and never publicly listed.
Does Peakmetric modify my TikTok account?
No. Peakmetric is read-only. We do not post, follow, like, or change anything on your TikTok account.
How do I delete my data?
Go to your account settings and click "Delete Account." All your data, including analytics history, will be permanently removed within 48 hours.
