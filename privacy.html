Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Peakmetric ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our influencer performance tracking platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we access the following through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Profile Information: Display name, username, avatar, bio, and verification status (via user.info.basic and user.info.profile)
  • Account Statistics: Follower count, following count, total likes, and video count (via user.info.stats)
  • Video Data: Public video details including view counts, likes, comments, and shares (via video.list)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to calculate engagement scores, track growth trends, analyze video performance, and generate reports. All data processing is for the sole purpose of providing analytics within the Peakmetric platform.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your performance reports are private to you and only shared when you choose to export or share them. Data may be disclosed only when legally required.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with industry-standard encryption and security measures. We retain data only while your account is active. Upon disconnection, your data is deleted from our systems within 30 days.

6. TikTok Data

Our use of TikTok data complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and API policies. Access is granted only through your explicit consent via Login Kit. You can revoke access anytime through your TikTok account settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Disconnect your TikTok account from Peakmetric to remove your data. For requests, contact support@peakmetric.app.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies only for session management. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are used.

9. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.
